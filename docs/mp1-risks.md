# Mini project 1 · Factory risk register

Write one risk per line: what could go wrong, and who gets hurt.

## Booking
A patient books a slot that another patient already has.
The copay shown before booking differs from the copay charged.
A cancelled visit still sends the patient a reminder.
A patient books a provider who does not accept their insurance plan.
A visit is booked in the wrong time zone.

## Refill
A refill is sent to the wrong pharmacy.
The refills left count does not go down after a refill.
A renewal request never reaches the provider.
A refill request is missing from the audit log.
A patient refills a prescription that belongs to someone else.
