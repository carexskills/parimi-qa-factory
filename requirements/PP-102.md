# PP-102 · Patient cancels a visit online before the cutoff

**Status:** Waiting for product owner

## Story

As a patient, I want to cancel online until two hours before my visit, so I can manage my booking and know when I need to call the front desk.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given a patient has a booked visit more than two hours away, when they cancel online, then the cancellation succeeds without a fee and the cancellation is recorded in the audit log with the cancelling patient's identity.
2. Given a patient has a booked visit less than two hours away, when they try to cancel online, then the cancellation does not occur and the patient sees the front desk phone number.
3. Given a visit has not started, when a front desk user cancels it, then the cancellation succeeds and the audit log identifies the front desk user who cancelled it.
4. Given a visit has started, when a patient or front desk user attempts cancellation, then the visit is not cancelled.
## Dependencies

- The booking system provides the visit start time and current visit status to cancellation processing.
- The system can determine whether an online cancellation is before the two-hour cutoff.
- The front desk phone number is available to display to patients blocked from online cancellation.
- Patient and front desk identities are available to the audit log for successful cancellations.
- Front desk users have a cancellation capability for visits that have not started.

## Decisions

- Online patient cancellation is available before the two-hour cutoff and has no fee.
- During the final two hours before a visit starts, the patient cannot cancel online and is shown the front desk phone number.
- Front desk staff can cancel any visit before it starts.
- Every cancellation is written to the audit log with who cancelled it.
- Only the cancellation rules change; other booking rules remain as they are.

## Open questions for the product owner

1. Is online cancellation allowed at exactly two hours before the visit, or does the restriction begin at that instant?
2. Which front desk phone number should the patient see, and should it be location-specific?
3. Which time zone defines the two-hour cutoff when visit, patient, or clinic time zones differ?
