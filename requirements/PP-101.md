# PP-101 · Patient books a visit and sees the copay

**Status:** Waiting for product owner

## Story
As a patient, I want to book a visit with a provider and see what I will pay, so I can get care without surprises.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given a provider has available appointment times, when the patient opens that provider's booking options, then the patient can see and select only open times in the future that start on the hour or half hour, are 30 minutes long, and do not fall on a weekend. Each time is shown in the clinic's time zone, with the zone named.
2. When booking a visit, the patient can select either an in-person or virtual visit and may enter a reason. If the reason is left empty, the booking stores "General visit".
3. Before the patient confirms the booking, the booking flow displays the amount the patient pays, not an estimate. For a Silver plan patient booking a standard visit, the displayed amount is $15.00 for either an in-person or virtual visit.
4. After a booking is confirmed, the confirmation displays the booked visit time in the clinic's named time zone and the same copay amount shown before confirmation. The receipt also shows that same copay amount.
5. A patient can cancel a booked visit any time before it starts, with no fee; after cancellation, the time becomes available again.
6. Two patients can never book the same provider at the same time. If a patient attempts to book a time that another patient has booked, the booking is not confirmed, the patient sees a clear message, and can pick another time.

## Dependencies
- Provider availability and appointment times are available to the booking flow.
- The patient's plan and its copay are available to the booking flow.
- The booking flow can save confirmed visits and prevent duplicate bookings for a time.
- A cancellation capability is available for booked visits.

## Decisions

- Appointment times: a patient can book only an open time in the future. Every visit is 30 minutes, starts on the hour or half hour, and is not on a weekend.
- Time zone: show every visit time in the clinic's time zone and name the zone.
- Copay: the displayed amount is what the patient pays, not an estimate. A Silver plan patient pays $15.00 for a standard visit, in person or virtual. Show the same amount before booking, on the confirmation, and on the receipt.
- Cancellation: a patient can cancel any time before the visit starts, with no fee. The time opens up again.
- Same time: two patients can never book the same provider at the same time. The second patient sees a clear message and picks another time.
- Reason: optional; if empty, the booking stores "General visit".

## Open questions for the product owner

1. What should the patient see or be able to do if their plan or copay cannot be determined before confirmation? The product owner specified the Silver plan amount, but did not address other plans or an unavailable plan/copay.
2. Should the patient receive a cancellation confirmation? The cancellation deadline, fee, and reopening behavior were answered, but confirmation to the patient was not.
