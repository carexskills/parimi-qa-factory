# PP-102 · Change request: late cancellations go to the front desk

Type: Change Request · Priority: High · Component: Patient portal / Booking
Reporter: Practice manager · Status: Ready for refinement

## Change
Today a patient can cancel online any time before the visit starts (BR-BOOK-03).
From now on, online cancellation closes 2 hours before the visit starts.
After that, the patient must call the front desk.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Until 2 hours before the visit starts, the patient can cancel online, with no fee.
2. In the last 2 hours, the patient cannot cancel online and sees the front desk phone number instead.
3. The front desk can still cancel any visit before it starts.
4. Every cancellation is written to the audit log, with who cancelled it.

## Notes
All patients, providers and data in ParimiPractice are synthetic.
Only the cancellation rules change. Do not change other booking rules.
