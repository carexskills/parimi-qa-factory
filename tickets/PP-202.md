# PP-202 · Patient signs in with a one-time code

Type: Story · Priority: High · Component: Patient portal / Sign-in
Reporter: Product owner · Status: Ready for refinement

## Story
As a patient, I want to sign in with my email, my password and a one-time code,
so that only I can see my health record.

## Acceptance criteria
1. With the right email and password, the patient is asked for a 6-digit one-time code.
2. The code is sent to the patient's email inbox and expires after 10 minutes.
3. A wrong password shows an error that does not reveal whether the email exists.
4. After 5 wrong attempts, the account is locked and the patient is told to contact the practice.
5. The sign-in page works in English and Spanish.

## Notes
All patients, providers and data in ParimiPractice are synthetic.
