# PP-201 · Patient requests a prescription refill

Type: Story · Priority: High · Component: Patient portal / Prescriptions
Reporter: Product owner · Status: Ready for refinement

## Story
As a patient, I want to request a refill of an active prescription from my portal,
so that I do not run out of my medicine.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Only an active prescription can be refilled.
2. If refills remain, the request is sent to the pharmacy, the status becomes
   "Refill requested", and the refills left go down by one.
3. If no refills remain, the status becomes "Renewal requested" and the patient
   is told that their provider has been asked to renew it.
4. Every refill or renewal request is written to the audit log.

## Notes
All patients, providers and prescriptions in ParimiPractice are synthetic.
