# PP-101 · Patient books a visit and sees the copay

Type: Story · Priority: High · Component: Patient portal / Booking
Reporter: Product owner · Status: Ready for refinement

## Story
As a patient, I want to book a visit with a provider and see what I will pay,
so that I can get care without surprises.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The patient sees the provider's open times and picks one.
2. The patient chooses an in-person or a virtual visit, and can add a reason.
3. Before confirming, the patient sees the copay for their plan.
4. The confirmation shows the visit time and the copay.
5. The patient can cancel the visit if their plans change.
6. Two patients should not end up with the same time.

## Notes
All patients, providers and data in ParimiPractice are synthetic.
