## Orphans

The following requirements have no corresponding business rule in `booking.rules.md`, so they are not represented as BR-traced cases:

- Appointment slots start on the hour or half hour, last 30 minutes, and do not fall on weekends.
- Every offered appointment time is displayed in the clinic timezone with the zone named; BR-BOOK-05 covers the confirmation only and does not require naming the zone.
- The patient can choose in-person or virtual visit types. BR-BOOK-04 specifies the Silver standard visit amount but not visit-type selection or parity by type.
- The pre-confirmation amount is explicitly the amount the patient pays, not an estimate.
- A rejected duplicate booking shows a clear message and lets the patient choose another time; BR-BOOK-02 only specifies refusal.
- Cancellation confirmation to the patient is an open product question.
- Behavior for other plans or an unavailable plan/copay is an open product question.

## Rule ambiguity

BR-BOOK-03 says cancellation is allowed “up to” the start time and its boundary says “through” the start time, but also says cancellation is refused “at or after” the start time. CASE-BOOK-03-03 follows the explicit refusal-at-start sentence; the exact-start boundary needs product-owner clarification.
