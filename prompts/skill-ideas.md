# Skills I want

One line each: the skill name, then what it does. Plain English.

spec-review: find gaps, vague words and missing boundaries in a spec, ranked by patient risk.
triage: read a failed test run and decide: test problem, environment problem, or suspected product defect.
jira-draft: draft a Jira bug from a suspected product defect, with steps, expected, actual and evidence.
risk-assess: score a change with impact and likelihood, and name its risk tier and approvers.
